# Release notes of version 66 (26.11.2026)

This text is the GitHub release v66.

**AIBody v66**: download `AIBody Companion Setup.exe`; it installs AIBody Companion and the package `d0n.AIBody.66.var` (the plugin, the AI-Buddy scene with its thumbnail and the Dollmaster assets) into your VaM folder. `d0n.AIBody.66.var`, `AI-Buddy.json` and `AI-Buddy.jpg` are attached too, for installing by hand.

**Install:** double-click `AIBody Companion Setup.exe` (SmartScreen: **More info** > **Run anyway**); it runs over v65. Remove older `d0n.AIBody.*.var` files from AddonPackages. Start **AIBody Companion** first, then VaM, and load **AI-Buddy** from `d0n.AIBody.66`.

**Changes**
- **Idle pose pool is a dropdown.** The checkbox *Idle poses from all pose files* is replaced by the dropdown **Idle pose pool** (tab AI Character > Idle poses) with two clear entries: **AIBody poses only** (default) uses the pose files in `Custom/Atom/Person/Pose/AIBody` plus the gestures.json poses, the poses of your pose commands and the Idle default pose; **All pose files** uses every pose file of your library, including other packages, plus those. The info text names the chosen pool and what it includes.
- **Old settings still work.** In `config.json` the new key is `body.idle_pose_pool` (`"aibody"` or `"all"`). An old `body.idle_poses_all_files` is read once: `true` becomes **All pose files**, `false` or missing becomes **AIBody poses only**; the new key wins when both exist. Scenes saved with the old checkbox still load (on = **All pose files**). An unknown value falls back to **AIBody poses only**.
- **Shorter idle pose interval.** The slider **Idle pose interval (s)** (`body.idle_pose_interval_s`) now goes down to 5 s (it was 30 s); the default stays 165 s and the maximum 18000 s. Each wait is still the interval plus or minus 25 % at random, so 5 s gives 3.75 to 6.25 s. That is longer than the time VaM needs to settle a pose, and a pose change that starts while she is still being put back keeps her original place, so idle poses do not overlap or starve. Saved values below 5 s are raised to 5 s.
- **Default idle pose interval is 30 s again** (it was 165 s in v59-v65), also after **Reset to Default**; a value you have saved is kept.
- **No lockout after your own pose.** After a pose command, **Apply Pose** or **Apply idle default pose**, idle poses used to be blocked for a fixed 120 s. That block is gone: your pose only restarts the normal idle wait (the interval).
- **The Idle default pose no longer points at a pose your character may not have.** The default is now the first menu entry **(first available pose)** (config `body.idle_pose_default` empty; it was `Stand Up`). It uses the first pose of the idle pool. **Apply idle default pose** and Reset to Default no longer fail with "this character has no pose 'Stand Up'": a saved name the character does not have falls back to the first available pose, and the note says so.
- **Expression and gesture controls removed from the menu.** The tab AI Character no longer has the section *Expression and gesture* (the **Expression** and **Gesture** dropdowns and the **Show expression**, **Reset expression** and **Play gesture** buttons), the status no longer shows an "Expression:" line, the Self-test no longer shows "Expression morphs" and "Timeline for ...", and the AI Character info text and the Reload Config JSON status no longer mention expressions. Nothing changes for the AI: expressions and gestures (nod for yes, head shake for no, gesture commands) work as before, `expressions.json` and `gestures.json` are still read and **Reload Config JSON** still reloads them. The trigger action "Reset expression" is gone; "Reload config files", "Expression: ..." and "Gesture: ..." remain. Old scenes and `config.json` files load without errors (these controls never stored a value).
- **Idle head motion and breathing are about twice as strong.** The sway angles are doubled (head 4 to 8 degrees, chest 2.5 to 5 degrees at intensity 1), the tempo is about twice as fast (weight shifts every 2.5 to 5.5 s instead of 5 to 11 s) and the breath is clearly visible (the breath morph swing is 2x, the chest and head lift per breath about 2x). **Idle intensity**, **Breathing**, **Breaths per minute** and **Breath depth** still scale it, and their defaults are unchanged.
- **Facial expressions of the AI fixed.** (1) Every morph now fades at its own speed: an old expression is gone in about the blend time of the next one (up to v65 one shared speed came from the newest, tiny change, so an old smile could take about 17 s to fade and faces mixed). VaM's own **Auto Expressions** stay paused until the fade has finished; if the character has no `AutoExpressions` storable, the log says so once. (2) When the AI sends no expression, the app's word-based fallback now sets at most one expression per answer, at least 4 s after the previous one, and never *sad* or *angry* (the AI can still choose them itself). (3) The *thinking* preset is a mild, symmetric look (both brows slightly up, inner brows up a little, mouth neutral) instead of a one-sided frown. **Reset Expression** is unchanged: it goes back to the look the scene had, not to zero.
- **Root reset after a pose now uses the place she had when the scene loaded.** With **Center body on control point** on, the hips' position is remembered once when the scene has loaded (and moves with **Body position**; **Reset poses** / **Reset body position** put it back). When a pose has settled, the whole body is moved by the same flat distance back to that place in one step (the pose's own offsets, all rotations and the height stay), with physics held for a moment so big moves do not whip the joints. If another person stands within 0.5 m of that place, it moves sideways just enough to clear them. A pose that throws the body more than 20 m away (or leaves a controller not-a-number or that far off) is skipped: the previous look is put back, the pose goes on a bad list for the session, and the log says so. Each reset writes one Info line (pose, delta, distance of the hips to the place, whether it moved sideways).
- **Note (from v65, not new):** a dropdown with fewer than two entries is shown as a read-only info line instead of a dropdown.
- **Installer note:** leave the optional **Grok Bot** task unchecked unless you use the Grok Bot bridge; it switches `ai.driver` to `grok_bot` (the default is the direct method).

Everything else is unchanged from v65.
